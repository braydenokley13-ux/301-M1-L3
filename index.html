Learning Objectives

📊 Diminishing Returns

Experience how additional spending yields decreasing marginal benefits. Learn to identify the point where spending more doesn't create proportional value.

📈 Efficiency Frontier

Visualize the frontier through real-time charts showing optimal spending levels. Understand when you're on, near, or past the efficiency frontier.

💰 Resource Constraints

Learn how different cap structures (no cap, hard cap, soft cap with tax) affect decision-making and strategic resource allocation.

⚖️ Trade-offs

Make tough choices between star players and roster balance. Understand opportunity cost through concrete player comparisons.
